## Design pivot recorded (S3 — measured, not assumed)

The planned scripted bound-crossing test under `#[tokio::test(start_paused = true)]` was PROVED impossible: tokio's paused auto-advance jumps virtual time to the next armed timer at EVERY park (standalone probes in `/tmp/opencode/pause_probe`: a single unix round trip only survives by `timeout_at`'s poll order; multi-round-trip chains HANG with elapsed +300 s). A socket-driven adapter pipeline cannot cross a 300 s window under a paused clock, and a real 305 s test would blow the 10-minute command budget. Final form (spec's sanctioned "fake clock"): the deadline wrapper `turn_deadline` extracted as a unit and crossed there (301 virtual seconds), with production wiring proven by `arming_a_request_suspends_the_bridge_budget` and by the two stderr assertions in S1's scripted test. Full decision rows: `decisions.md` 2026-10-02T12:2xZ (S3).
